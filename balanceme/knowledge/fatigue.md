# Fatigue in chronic illness (sample content – replace with your institution's material)

Fatigue is one of the most common symptoms in chronic illness such as rheumatoid arthritis, multiple sclerosis, lupus and long-term pain conditions. It differs from ordinary tiredness because rest does not fully relieve it.

Pacing means spreading activities across the day and week, alternating demanding tasks with rest, and avoiding a boom-and-bust pattern where a good day is followed by several bad ones.

Patients should contact their doctor if fatigue suddenly gets much worse, comes with fever, weight loss, shortness of breath or new pain, as this can signal a flare, infection, anaemia or a medication side effect.
